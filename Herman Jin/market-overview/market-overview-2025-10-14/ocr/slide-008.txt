JPMorgan Chase Announces
$1.5 Trillion Plan for U.S.
Critical Industries
JPMorgan Chase unveiled a 10-year, $1.5 trillion
Security and Resiliency Initiative on Monday to
finance investments in U.S. sectors essential for
economic security, including advanced
manufacturing, defense, energy, and strategic
technologies like Al and quantum computing. The
plan features up to $10 billion in direct equity and
venture capital for domestic companies to
| mitigate supply chain risks and foreign
dependencies. CEO Jamie Dimon stressed the
need to strengthen U.S, production of critical
materials amid geopolitical challenges.
